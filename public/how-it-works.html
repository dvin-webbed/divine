Process

From account setup to your first payout

Create an account, add funds, then choose your principal and payout interval from the dashboard.

01

Create your account

Complete the short profile so your account is ready to receive funds.

02

Add funds

Use an available funding method to add cash to your account.

03

Choose your amount and interval

Your principal selects the configured monthly rate tier. Choose bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, bi-annually, or annually.

04

Review the estimate

The dashboard shows the applied monthly rate and estimated payout for your chosen interval before you invest.

05

Receive payouts in cash

When processed, payouts are credited to your available in-app cash balance. Withdraw it or invest it again manually.

The engine behind it

One rate tier, scaled to your chosen interval

Monthly profit is estimated by multiplying principal by its configured rate. Payout intervals scale that estimate by 0.5, 1, 3, 6, or 12.

All rates and payout figures are illustrative and are not guaranteed returns.
