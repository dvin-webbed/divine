Privacy Policy

Placeholder legal text for this prototype. Replace with counsel-reviewed language before handling any real personal data.

What this prototype actually collects. To be transparent about the current build: the signup form collects name, email, and a hashed password. The site logs anonymous page views (path, referrer, browser user-agent, and a salted one-way hash of your IP address — never the IP itself) to the site's internal monitoring dashboard.

What belongs here in production. A real privacy policy needs to disclose every category of data collected (including anything from Plaid, KYC/identity verification providers, or analytics tools you add), how long it's retained, who it's shared with, user rights under applicable law (CCPA, GDPR if you have EU users), and a real contact method for data requests.

Before launch. Have this reviewed by counsel, and keep it in sync with what the codebase actually does — a privacy policy that doesn't match your real data flows is itself a compliance risk.
