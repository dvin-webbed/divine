Glossary

Terms you'll run into across the site, defined in plain language.

PipThe smallest standard unit of price movement — Blue Pips borrows the term to signal precision in how positions are tracked, not that the platform trades currency pairs.
DistributionA scheduled cash payout estimate based on principal, configured monthly tier rate, and withdrawal interval.
Reserve bufferThe liquid balance receiving 10% of recorded gross yield, used to fund due payouts when sufficient.
Observed source splitThe actual gross yield percentages recorded from DeFi and arbitrage over the dashboard's reporting window.
Target yieldAn estimated, not guaranteed, monthly rate assigned by the principal tier.
