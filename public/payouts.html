Payouts are credited to available cash

When a payout is processed, it moves to your in-app cash balance. You can withdraw available cash or invest it again manually; reinvestment is not automatic.

Common questions

Payout FAQ

How is the payout estimate calculated?

The principal selects a monthly rate tier. Payout factors are 0.5× bi-weekly, 1× monthly, 3× quarterly, 6× bi-annually, and 12× annually.

Can I change my payout interval?

Choose an interval for a new investment from the dashboard. Adding to an existing Tiered Income position updates its payout interval.

Where are payouts credited?

Processed payouts are credited to your in-app cash balance. You can withdraw or invest that cash manually.

Is any of this guaranteed?

No. Rates, payout estimates, and schedules on this prototype are illustrative and do not guarantee a return.
