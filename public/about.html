Built by people tired of watching from outside

Blue Pips Investment is a prototype for a single Tiered Income product, with monthly rates selected by principal and scheduled payouts credited from a reserve.

The short version

Private markets have historically been priced, structured, and gated for institutions — not individuals. Blue Pips exists to run the same underwriting, servicing, and reporting discipline those institutions use, but open an account to anyone who wants exposure without a seven-figure minimum.

We're a small, deliberately technical team: the same people who build the underwriting models also build the software that reports on them, which is what lets the platform move faster than a traditional fund structure.

1

Direct, not pooled-blind

Every position on the platform is individually underwritten and visible in your dashboard — not folded into an opaque fund-of-funds.

2

Software-run operations

Sourcing, servicing, and reporting run through the same platform, which is what keeps fees low relative to traditional private funds.

3

Built for the long game

The interface focuses on one configured rate model and clear reporting of observed yield sources and reserve balances.
